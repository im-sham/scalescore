# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the ScaleScore community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent while being direct about technical tradeoffs.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus critiques on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Help keep newcomer paths clear and documented.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or community discussions.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy.

---

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers via the repository owners listed in `README.md`.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
